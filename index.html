The problem

Every team is racing to ship AI features. Prompt injection, system-prompt leakage, secret disclosure, jailbreaks, and over-permissioned agents are shipping right alongside them. Most teams have no way to test for any of it before it hits production.

What Aegis checks

Prompt injection

Direct and indirect injection that overrides your system instructions (LLM01).

System prompt leakage

Attempts to extract your hidden instructions and configuration (LLM07).

Sensitive disclosure

Coaxing out API keys, secrets, and private data (LLM02).

Jailbreaks

Role-play and DAN-style attacks that bypass your guardrails (LLM01).

Improper output handling

Model output that becomes stored XSS or injection downstream (LLM05).

Excessive agency

Agents taking destructive actions they should never be allowed to (LLM06).

How it works

Point Aegis at your AI endpoint. No SDK, no code changes. It works against any model (Anthropic, OpenAI, or your own).
Aegis runs the attack suite with benign canary tokens. Nothing harmful is generated.
You get a 0 to 100 security score, a pass/fail per OWASP category, and specific remediation for each finding.
Re-run on every release. Catch regressions before your users (or attackers) do.
